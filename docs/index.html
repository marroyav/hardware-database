How To Read The Diagrams

Reading Guide

Hardware

Mechanical assemblies, structures, boxes, frames, racks, drawers, and installed physical units.

Electronics

Active boards, readout cards, controllers, power modules, switches, and digitization hardware.

Connector / Interface

Flanges, connector-bearing boundaries, warm/cold interfaces, and other typed connection surfaces. In these models, this category stands in for connectors and interface panels.

Cable

Electrical cables and bundled cable objects that connect subsystems or carry power and signals.

Fiber

Optical fiber paths used for timing, data transport, or calibration distribution.

Sensor

Instrumentation endpoints such as RTDs, level meters, gas arrays, and other sensing elements.

Batch

Batch-tracked or type-tracked parts where the model currently represents a production family rather than a serialized individual item.

Solid arrows mean assembly or containment.

Dashed arrows mean functional links such as cabling, interfaces, timing, power, or readout dependence.

Subsystem boxes group related component types. The dense detector plate uses these groupings to approximate the presentation-style hierarchy sheets.
